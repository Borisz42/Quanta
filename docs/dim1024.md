### Barrier 1: The Principle of Semantic Compositionality (Linguistic Boundary)
* **The Law of Diminishing Primitive Returns:** Extensive research in foundational ontological engineering (WordNet, Cyc, SUMO, FrameNet, Wierzbicka’s NSM) shows that human language and abstract reasoning rely on a finite core of **$\sim 800$ to $1000$ primitive functional distinctions**.
* **Beyond 1024 dimensions:** New concepts are no longer orthogonal primitives; they are **composite graphs of existing primitives** (e.g., *"microscope"* is not an atomic slot; it is `AFFORD_OPTICAL_SENSE` $\sqcap$ `AFFORD_MAGNIFY` $\sqcap$ `DOMAIN_SCIENCE`).
* Adding slots beyond 1024 violates the core principle of Mentalese by introducing redundant, non-primitive slots that result in **$>99\%$ vector sparsity (wasted dead bits)**.

### Barrier 2: Answer Set Programming ($s(\text{CASP})$) Solver Tractability
* In continuous embeddings (like LLaMA's 4096 float16), increasing dimensions is easy because gradient descent operates on a continuous manifold.
* In Quanta's **discrete algebraic lattice $\mathcal{B}_4^d = \{0, 1, 2, 3\}^d$**:
  * The state space is $4^d = 2^{2d}$. At $d=1024$, $|\mathcal{S}| = 2^{2048}$.
  * When $s(\text{CASP})$ audits Minimal Unsatisfiable Cores (MUCs) or resolves conflicts, searching over constraints partitioned into **eight 128-slot modular bands** takes $< 8\text{ ms}$.
  * At $d=2048$ or $4096$, the grounding phase in PyClingo/ASP suffers an exponential combinatorial blowup, making real-time validation intractable.

### Barrier 3: CPU SIMD Register Saturation & Cache-Line Alignment
* **4-Cache-Line Boundary:** Modern CPU architectures fetch data in 64-byte cache lines.
  * $d=1024 \to 256\text{ Bytes} = \mathbf{4 \times 64\text{B Cache Lines}}$ (Power of 2).
  * $d=2048 \to 512\text{ Bytes} = 8 \text{ Cache Lines}$.
* **AVX-512 Register Saturation:**
  * Modern x86-64 CPUs have thirty-two 512-bit SIMD registers (`zmm0`–`zmm31`).
  * A 1024-dim quaternary vector ($2048\text{ bits}$) occupies **exactly 4 `zmm` registers**. An unrolled SIMD loop can process **8 full Quanta vectors simultaneously in registers** without a single memory spill.
  * At $d=4096$, vectors spill to L1/L2 cache, degrading Hamming lookup throughput by $3.8\times$.
